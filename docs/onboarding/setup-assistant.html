What is new here

What the setup assistant adds to the app. The requirements are in docs/onboarding/PRD.md; where this mockup and that document differ, the document is right.

  • NewThe assistant itself: a full-window sequence after “Create your sign-in” on a new installation, with no side pane until it ends.
  • NewLogins are added inside the assistant, with the same three checks as Add credential. The tiles say which provider is already added, and that Gemini builds but does not review yet.
  • NewAgents are made automatically: one General agent for each login, named after it. Today the owner connects each agent in Agents.
  • New“I only have one subscription” lets the owner continue with one provider, and says where finished work will wait.
  • NewA first-task picker: three built-in starter tasks, or one the owner writes.
  • New“Set up later” on every screen, and “Skip, take me to the task board” on the last. Both open the task board with the setup checklist and its spotlight, which is the other mockup, checklist-spotlight.html.
